Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 29, 2026

Last Updated: May 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to On Pace For ("we," "our," or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our iOS application (Bundle ID: com.timhibbard.onpacefor).

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Health and Fitness Data

With your explicit permission, On Pace For accesses the following data from Apple HealthKit:

  • Step count
  • Exercise minutes and workout data
  • Calories burned
  • Distance traveled
  • Active energy

Important: All health data is stored locally on your device only. We do not upload, transmit, or store your health data on external servers or in the cloud.

2.2 Motion and Activity Data

The App may access motion and activity data from your device's sensors to track physical activity and improve accuracy of fitness projections.

2.3 Analytics Data

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand app usage and improve performance. This may include:

  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App usage patterns (features used, session duration)
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data

This data does not include your personal health information and is anonymized.

2.4 Subscription Information

If you purchase a subscription through Apple In-App Purchase, we receive basic transaction information from Apple (subscription status, purchase date). Your payment information is processed entirely by Apple and never shared with us.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Health Data: To display your activity progress, calculate projections, and provide personalized fitness insights. This data never leaves your device.
  • Analytics: To improve app performance, identify bugs, and understand feature usage.
  • Subscription Management: To provide premium features and manage your subscription status.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: All health and fitness data is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's secure storage mechanisms.
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not store your health data on external servers or cloud services.
  • Device Security: Your data is protected by your device's security features (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID).

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal health information with third parties. The only third-party services we use are:

  • Firebase (Google): For analytics and crash reporting (anonymized data only)
  • Apple: For in-app purchase processing and subscription management

Neither service receives your health data. For more information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • HealthKit Permissions: You can revoke access to HealthKit data at any time through iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → On Pace For.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app from your device. Since all data is stored locally, uninstalling removes all your information.
  • Analytics Opt-Out: You can limit analytics tracking through iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

7. Children's Privacy

On Pace For is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Advertising

On Pace For does not display advertisements. We do not share your data with advertising networks.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or by updating the "Last Updated" date above. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
