Questions

Can I try Pro first?

Yes. Watch a short ad in the app and every Pro feature is unlocked for 24 hours. The subscriptions themselves don't have a free trial.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

In the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then On Pace For. Pro stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Nothing. It's in Apple Health and on your iPhone, not on a server. You keep all the free features.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do prices change by country?

Yes. Apple sets the local price in each country, so it may differ from the US prices above.
