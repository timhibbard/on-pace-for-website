Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free trial work?

When you subscribe, you get 14 days of full premium access at no cost. You won't be charged until the trial ends. Cancel anytime during the trial to avoid being charged.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can cancel anytime through your Apple ID subscription settings. You'll keep premium access until the end of your current billing period.

How do I manage my subscription?

Go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your iOS device. Find "On Pace For" to view, modify, or cancel your subscription.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

All your data stays on your device. You'll lose access to premium features, but your historical data and basic tracking continue to work.

Are there any additional fees?

No hidden fees. The price you see is what you pay. Subscriptions are processed through Apple, so prices may vary by region based on your App Store location.

Can I get a refund?

Refund requests are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.
