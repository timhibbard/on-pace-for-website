Need Help with the App?

Subscription Issues

Manage subscriptions through iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com

Data Not Syncing?

Make sure you've granted HealthKit permissions: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → On Pace For

App Crashes

Try restarting your device, updating to the latest iOS version, and reinstalling the app. If issues persist, contact us with details.
