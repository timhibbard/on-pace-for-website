Common problems

My numbers are zero or don't match the Fitness app

The app can only read what you allow in Apple Health. Open the Settings app, go to Privacy & Security, then Health, then On Pace For, and turn on every category.

Subscriptions and refunds

Subscriptions are managed in the Settings app under your name, then Subscriptions. Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The app crashes

Make sure you're on the latest version from the App Store. If it still happens, email me and say what screen you were on.
