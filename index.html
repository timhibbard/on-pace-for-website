Know where your day will finish.

On Pace For looks at your steps, exercise minutes, and Move calories so far and works out where you'll end up tonight, this week, this month, and this year. If you're falling behind, it tells you how much it takes each hour to catch up.

Download on the App Store

Free. Pro is $0.99 a month or $9.99 a year, and starts with a 14-day free trial.

How it works

Today

Each goal shows what you've done, where you're on pace to finish when your active hours end, and the goal itself. If one is slipping, you'll see what it takes per hour to get it back.

Week, month, and year

Your totals are compared with last week, last month, or last year, and with the last 7, 31, or 365 days, so you can tell a good stretch from a slow one.

Works with your watch

It reads from Apple Health, so it works with an Apple Watch or any watch or app that saves there, including Garmin and COROS. Your Health data stays on your iPhone.

On Pace For Pro

Pro adds route maps, splits, and pace charts for your workouts, heart rate and power zones, workout recaps written by Apple Intelligence on iPhones that support it, year projections, custom timelines, trend graphs, bigger widgets, data export, and no ads. It's $0.99 a month or $9.99 a year, and new subscribers get the first 14 days free.
