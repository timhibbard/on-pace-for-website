What On Pace For does

It tells you where you'll finish, and what it takes to get there.

Today

Steps, exercise minutes, and Move calories each get a card with what you've done so far, where you're on pace to finish when your active hours end, and your goal. A chip says whether you're on pace, close, or behind.

If you're behind, a catch-up card works out what you'd need each hour to still make it. Steps update live as you walk.

Week, month, and year

Each one shows where you're headed and how it compares with the last full week, month, or year, and with the last 7, 31, or 365 days. Arrows show whether you're up or down.

Pick the workouts you care about, like running or cycling, and you'll see projected miles and time for those too. You can also choose which day your week starts.

Workouts

Your workouts from the last 30 days, with distance, pace, calories, heart rate, and elevation. Anything that saves workouts to Apple Health shows up, not just Apple Watch.

Some watches don't record Apple's exercise minutes. On those days, your workout time counts toward your exercise goal instead. Court and studio sports like pickleball or yoga are tracked by time.

Without opening the app

Widgets on the Home Screen and Lock Screen, a Live Activity in the Dynamic Island, Control Center controls, and status notifications at the times you pick. There's an Apple Watch app with complications, and you can ask Siri "Am I on pace for my step goal?"

On iPhone Duo, the layout fits both the closed and the open screen.

Pro

$0.99 a month or $9.99 a year. Watch a short ad in the app to try it free for 24 hours.

Maps, splits, and pace

Your route on a map, mile or kilometer splits, and a pace chart where faster splits stand taller.

Heart rate and power zones

How long you spent in each zone, using your Health zone settings if you've set them.

Workout recaps

A short written recap of each workout, generated on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence where it's supported.

Year and timelines

Projections for the whole year, plus your own timelines for a training block, a challenge, or any dates you like.

Trend graphs

Each goal charted through the day, week, month, or year, with where it's projected to end up.

Bigger widgets, export, no ads

Medium and large widgets, CSV export of your data, and no ads.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad with iOS 27 or later. The Apple Watch app needs watchOS 26.2 or later.
  • Access to Apple Health (steps, exercise, Move, workouts, heart rate) and, for live steps, Motion & Fitness. Notifications are optional.
  • Projections work offline. Your Health data stays on your device.
