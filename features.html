Everything On Pace For Does

Know where you'll finish, and what it takes to get there

Free Features

📊 Today, At a Glance

For steps, exercise minutes, and Move calories, see what you've done so far, where you're on pace to finish by the end of your active hours, and your goal.

  • On pace, close, or behind for each goal
  • Catch-up targets: what it takes each hour to reach a goal
  • Live step counts that update as you move
  • Settings, Day / Week / Month / Year, and timelines at the bottom of the screen

📈 Week, Month, and Year

See where this week, month, and year are on pace to finish, and how that compares with the last full period and with the last 7, 31, or 365 days.

  • Comparisons with arrows showing which way you're trending
  • Projected miles and time for the workouts you care about, like running or cycling
  • Choose the day your week starts

🏃 Your Workouts

Browse your workouts from the last 30 days with distance, pace, calories, heart rate, and elevation.

  • Works with Apple Watch and other watches and apps that save workouts to Apple Health
  • Workout time counts toward your exercise on days your watch doesn't record exercise minutes
  • Court, studio, and team sports like pickleball, yoga, and soccer are tracked by time

⌚ Everywhere You Look

Check your pace without opening the app.

  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
  • A Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
  • Control Center controls
  • An Apple Watch app with complications
  • Status notifications at the times you choose
  • Siri and Shortcuts: "Am I on pace for my step goal?"
  • Designed for iPhone Duo, on both the closed and open screens
